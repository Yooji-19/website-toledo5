Why I Want to Visit Honshu

Honshu Island is home to the iconic Mount Fuji, one of the most beautiful and recognizable landmarks in the world. Seeing Mount Fuji in person has always been one of my biggest dreams.

I want to experience the peaceful atmosphere, traditional Japanese culture, and the breathtaking scenery surrounding the mountain. I also hope to visit Lake Kawaguchi and explore nearby towns. Standing in front of Mt. Fuji would be an unforgettable moment in my life.
