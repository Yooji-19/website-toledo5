Why I Want to Visit Shinjuku

Shinjuku is one of the most vibrant and energetic districts in Tokyo. I want to visit the famous stairs featured in the anime movie Kimi no Na wa (Your Name). Seeing that real-life location would feel nostalgic and surreal.

Aside from that, I also want to explore Don Quijote, neon-lit streets, arcades, and experience the nightlife. Shinjuku represents the modern, bright, and cinematic side of Japan.
